# Find Planet X

Find the new planet. It is 1929, you are the farm boy Lowell Observatory just hired, and the ninth planet is a faint point that moves between two glass plates.
Expose the plates on clear nights, label them by hand, develop them, blink the pairs under the comparator and check every suspect against a third plate.
Nothing is remembered that is not written in the notebook. Play at https://thevangelist.github.io/find-planet-x/ or run `./serve.sh` and open http://localhost:8765.
Credits for images, sounds and the star catalogue are in CREDITS.md.
